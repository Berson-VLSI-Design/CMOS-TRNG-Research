# Research methodology

## Semester V: Ring-oscillator TRNG

1. Develop and characterize a CMOS inverter in Cadence Virtuoso with the gpdk90 technology library.
2. Build conventional CMOS ring oscillators and investigate tetrahedral, pentagonal and hexagonal interconnected variants.
3. Study current-starved and dynamic-CMOS ring oscillators.
4. Compare supply-voltage-dependent frequency, power and total harmonic distortion in circuit simulation.
5. Use a D flip-flop and clock to sample oscillator behaviour.
6. Perform full-custom layout and report DRC, LVS, parasitic extraction and post-layout analysis for the dynamic-CMOS implementation.

## Semester VI: Chaotic current-mode TRNG

1. Study CDTA terminal behaviour and transistor-level implementation, including current-differencing and transconductance stages.
2. Verify the building block with a KHN filter.
3. Develop a CDTA-based multiscroll chaotic oscillator, including nonlinear function and state-space analysis.
4. Inspect transient waveforms, phase portraits and FFT characteristics.
5. Buffer the sensitive oscillator output, apply Schmitt-trigger hysteresis and clock a D flip-flop to extract bits.
6. Evaluate statistical properties of the resulting simulated bitstream and explore DSSS/BPSK links and next-bit prediction tests.

## Evidence boundary

This methodology summarizes the two submitted academic reports. Figures and metrics are reported simulation results, not independent measurement or silicon characterization. The original reports should be consulted for circuit diagrams and assumptions.
